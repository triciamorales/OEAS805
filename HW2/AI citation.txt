what does this mean?

This is a FutureWarning from Seaborn (not a breaking error). Your code still ran and generated the plot, but Seaborn is telling you that passing palette without defining hue is deprecated and will break in future updates (version 0.14.0+).

How to Fix It
Since your bars are grouped by the y-axis (y='area'), assign hue='area' and set legend=False inside sns.barplot:

Python
sns.barplot(
    data=area_summary, 
    x='total_items_collected', 
    y='area', 
    hue='area', 
    palette='Blues_r', 
    legend=False
)
Why this happens
In newer versions of Seaborn, color palettes are reserved for grouping variables assigned to hue. By explicitly assigning hue='area' and hiding the unnecessary legend with legend=False, you get the exact same colored bar plot without the red warning box.
